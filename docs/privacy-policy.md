# Privacy Policy — Schoology Grade Tracker

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

Schoology Grade Tracker ("the app") helps students see and forecast their own
grades. This policy says what the app stores and where. The same text is in the
app under Settings → Privacy (`mobile/src/app/privacy.tsx`); keep the two in step.

## Your account

The app needs an account. When you sign in (with email, Google, or Apple), we
store your name, email address, username and gradebook (classes, categories,
assignments, scores, forecasts and upcoming work) on our server, which runs on
Supabase. This lets your grades sync to your other devices. Each account can
only read its own data. A copy is kept on your phone so the app keeps working
without internet; signing out removes that copy.

## Screenshots and saved reports

Screenshots and saved Schoology pages are read on your phone. The pictures and
files are never uploaded; only the grades you choose to save are kept.

## Schoology calendar link

Your calendar link works like a password. It is kept in your phone's secure
storage and is only ever sent to Schoology, to read your calendar. It is not
stored on our server.

## What we never do

We never ask for your Schoology password. The app has no ads, no tracking and no
analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Erase all data** removes your grades (from every device when signed in).
- **Settings → Delete account** permanently deletes your account and everything
  in it, on every device.
- **Without the app:** email **thkim9989@gmail.com** from the address your
  account uses and ask for it to be deleted. We delete the account and
  everything in it within 30 days.

## Children

The app is meant for high-school students. It does not knowingly collect more
than the account details listed above.
