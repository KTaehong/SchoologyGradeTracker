# Grade Tracker — Support

Need help, found a bug, or want your data deleted? Email
**thkim9989@gmail.com**. We answer within a few days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Without an account your grades are saved only on your phone. An account
(Sign in with Apple, Google, or email) keeps them in sync between devices.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap **Forgot password?**. Open the
link in the email on the same phone, then choose a new password.

**My confirmation or reset link says it expired.**
Links work once and expire after an hour. Ask for a new one from the sign-in
screen, and open it on the phone where you asked for it.

**Does the app need my Schoology password?**
Never. You type your grades, scan a screenshot, or open a saved grades page.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
In the app: **Settings → Account & sync → Delete account**. This permanently
deletes your account and every class, grade and forecast in it.
Can't open the app? Email us from the address on your account and we will
delete it within 30 days.

**How do I erase the grades on this phone?**
**Settings → Data → Erase all data.**
